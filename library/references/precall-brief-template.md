# Pre-Call Brief Template

Every pre-call brief is **2 pages max**. Cut hard: if a line does not change what the AE says or asks on the call, delete it.

## Format
- The seller's approved internal doc format (set per deployment). Default: white background, one sans-serif typeface, large title, short section headers, compact body text, arrow lists (`→  `), no bullets.
- Share scope: exactly who the requester names, nobody else.

## Structure (this order, about 40 content lines)
1. **Header block**: title `PRE-CALL BRIEF: <COMPANY>: <TOPIC>`, then generated date, meeting time in UTC and local zones, read time, confidentiality.
2. **COMPANY SNAPSHOT**: 3-4 lines. Industry, size, HQ, ownership (only if it matters), ICP fit, CRM status.
3. **MEETING CONTEXT**: 2-3 lines. How we met, booking or reschedule history if it signals something, our attendees, goal.
4. **ATTENDEES**: one line per person: name, title, why they matter. Partners in one line.
5. **PAIN POINTS TO ANCHOR ON**: 3 lines max, only the sharpest.
6. **DEMO FOCUS**: 3 lines max. What to lead with, what language to use, what to stay away from.
7. **KEY QUESTIONS TO ASK**: 4 max.
8. **LIKELY OBJECTIONS**: 3 max, each with a one-line counter.
9. **RECOMMENDED PLAY**: one paragraph, 3-4 sentences. The call strategy.
10. **KEY FACTS TO HAVE READY**: 3 lines max, only facts likely to come up.

## Sourcing checklist (before writing)
- CRM contact and company record (properties, owner, lifecycle, last touch)
- CRM engagements (recent emails, notes, meetings; read the actual bodies for tone and commitments)
- Calendar event (attendees, acceptance status, description)
- Account-knowledge store (`account-knowledge/<company>.md`) if it exists
- Links the requester supplied (fetch them)
- Prior briefs for the same account: update, do not duplicate

## Voice
- No em dashes, no AI slop, direct and specific. Write like a sharp SE's prep notes, not a research report.
- Customer name isolation: never name another customer anywhere in the brief.
