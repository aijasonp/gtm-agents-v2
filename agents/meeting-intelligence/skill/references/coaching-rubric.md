# Coaching rubric

Coach one or two things per call. Specific, kind, and tied to a moment in the transcript.

| Area | Good | Coach when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Talk ratio | Your side under 60% | Over 65% |
| Discovery | 5 or more open questions | Fewer than 3 |
| Monologue | No turn over 150 words | Any turn over 250 words |
| Next step | Agreed, dated, with an owner | None heard |
| MEDDPICC | New evidence on at least two elements | Stage needs elements the call never touched |
| Objections | Acknowledged, then a question | Answered with a feature list |

Output:

```
Coaching for Sam Rivera on Contoso proposal review
1. Talk ratio 72%. Try pausing after the price and asking what it is being compared to.
2. Lee raised budget timing. Next time ask what happens if this waits a quarter.
Missed areas for a Proposal stage deal: economic buyer, paper process
```
