# Reply patterns

Short. One idea, one ask. Use only facts in `company.yaml`.

## Interested
Thank them in one line. Offer two days or the calendar link. Nothing else.

## Objection: competitor
Accept it. Say what teams usually add alongside, using one value-prop outcome. Offer a short side-by-side.

## Objection: price
Acknowledge it. Tie cost to one pain from the value props. Ask if a 15 minute look at the math is worth it.

## Objection: timing
Accept it. Ask permission to check back next quarter.

## Objection: no need
Thank them for the clear answer and close it. Leave the calendar link. No question.

## Referral intro
Name who referred you in the first line. One sentence on what you do. One ask.

## Question
Answer with the approved fact only, then offer a short call. If the facts do not cover it, the owner answers by hand.

## Never
- Never push back on an opt-out or reply to it with anything but silence and the stop.
- Never open with a filler follow-up line. The Copy QA Gate banned list applies to every reply.
- Never claim a result that is not a proof point in `company.yaml`.
