# Recap format

Sent to buyer attendees within a few hours of the call. Under 170 words.

```
Subject: Recap: <meeting title>
Hi <first name>,

Thanks for the time today. What I heard:
- <pain or goal in their words>
- <what matters in the decision>

Next steps:
- <owner>: <action> by <date>

Did I miss anything?
<sender>
```

Rules:
- Only what was said on the call. No new claims, no pricing that was not discussed.
- Next steps name an owner and a date when one was said. Otherwise leave the date out.
- One question at the end.

## CRM note

Same facts, internal voice: attendees, what we learned, risks heard, next steps with dates.

## Tasks

One task per next step that belongs to our side. Due on the date said on the call, else two business days after the call.
