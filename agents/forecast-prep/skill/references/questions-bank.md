# Questions a manager asks on a commit deal

One line per trigger, `key: question`. The agent picks the 2 or 3 that match the deal. Priority: close date passed, slips, next step, high risk, then MEDDPICC gaps in the order listed.

no_next_step: What is the next dated step with the buyer, and who owns it?
next_step_overdue: The next step date passed. Did it happen, and what moved?
slipped: The close date moved before. What is different this time that makes this date real?
slipped_twice: This deal slipped more than once. Should it stay in commit, or move to best case?
economic_buyer: Who signs, and have you met them directly?
paper_process: What does legal, security and procurement need, and is that started?
decision_process: Walk me through every step from here to a signature, with dates.
champion: Who is selling this internally when you are not in the room?
metrics: What number does the buyer expect to change, and did they say it themselves?
decision_criteria: What will they judge us on, and where do we stand on each?
competition: Who else is in the deal, and why do we win?
identify_pain: What breaks for them if they do nothing this quarter?
high_risk: Deal Inspection rates this deal high risk. What do you know that the data does not show?
close_date_passed: The close date already passed. What is the new date and why?
