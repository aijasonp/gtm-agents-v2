# Forecast call formats

## Definitions

- **Commit**: open deals the rep put in the commit category, closing this quarter.
- **Best case**: commit plus best-case deals closing this quarter (the realistic ceiling).
- **Pipeline**: every open deal closing this quarter, any category except omitted.
- **Won**: closed won with a close date this quarter.
- **Suggested commit**: commit deals whose Deal Inspection risk is under the threshold (50), plus best-case deals in a late stage with risk under 25. Deals never inspected count, and are marked "not inspected".
- **Variance**: rep commit minus suggested commit. Flagged when it is more than 25% and $25,000 apart. Rep above suggested reads as "happy ears", rep below reads as "sandbagging".
- **Linear pace**: quota times the share of the quarter that has passed. Attainment ahead of or behind pace is won minus pace.

## AE prep (one message per rep)

```
Forecast prep: Sam Rivera | Q4, week of Oct 19
Quarter: won $55,000 | commit $140,000 | best case $236,000 | pipeline $236,000
Month: commit $140,000 | best case $140,000
Your commit $140,000 | suggested $0 (Contoso Health dropped: risk 77)
Changes since last week: Contoso Health moved into commit; Northwind close date Dec 1 -> Dec 15
Commit deals:
- Contoso Health - Agents $140,000 | Proposal | risk 77
  Q: The close date already passed. What is the new date and why?
  Q: Who signs, and have you met them directly?
```

## Manager prep

One line per rep: quota, won, commit, suggested, variance flag, coverage. Then the top deals to inspect, deals that slipped twice or more, and three summary bullets.

## Exec prep

Org won against quota and linear pace, commit and best case, week-over-week change in each, biggest swings, top risks, three summary bullets.

## Rules

- Every number comes from the tools. Never round a number differently from the tool output.
- A missing quota or category is stated, not guessed.
- Questions are for the call, not judgments of the rep.
