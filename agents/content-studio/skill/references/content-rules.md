# Content rules

## Sources

- Themes come from the insights table (win and loss reasons, objections, competitor mentions, pains) from the last 30 days.
- Only the theme is used. Account names, people and quotes from calls never appear in public content.
- Proof points come from `company.yaml` only.

## LinkedIn posts

- Hook on line one, under 12 words.
- Short lines, a blank line between ideas.
- One question at the end. At most two hashtags.
- Under 180 words. Passes the Copy QA Gate (32 of 40).

## Short video

| Part | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Hook | a question, at most 3 seconds (7 words at 2.4 words per second) |
| Beats | 2 to 4, one point each |
| CTA | one ask |
| Length | total words at most seconds x 2.4 |

## Captions

- Lines up to 32 characters, never split a word.
- Up to 2 lines per cue.
- Cue time is the cue's share of the words times the video length.

## On-screen text frames

- Inside the safe zone: 10 percent from each side, clear of the bottom 15 percent (platform buttons and captions sit there).
- Lines up to 32 characters.
- On screen at least 0.3 seconds per word.
- No banned words and no dashes.
