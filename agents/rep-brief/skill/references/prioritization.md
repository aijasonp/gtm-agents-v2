# Prioritization

Rank by how fast the value of acting goes away.

| Rank | Action | Why first |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Positive reply or meeting request | Replies cool within hours |
| 2 | Hot lead past or near SLA | Speed to lead decides most inbound deals |
| 3 | Referral, objection or question reply | Someone is waiting on you |
| 4 | Push-button an A-tier account with a fresh, mentionable signal and no open deal | Signal value decays in weeks |
| 5 | Fix a deal Deal Inspection flagged (highest risk first) | Pipeline you already have |
| 6 | Set a dated next step on deals missing one | Cheap and stops slips |
| 7 | Close the pipeline gap: push-button the top accounts | Quota math |

Duplicates collapse. The brief shows the top three.

## Account ranking

Signal Scout score first (tier A, B, C, D; out-of-ICP accounts never appear), then ICP fit for unscored accounts at or above the ICP floor.

## Contact ranking

Not touched recently, then engaged with your content, then seniority, then buyer over champion.
