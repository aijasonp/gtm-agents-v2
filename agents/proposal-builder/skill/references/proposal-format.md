# Proposal format

1. Header: company name or logo, quote id, valid-until date.
2. Title: Proposal for the account. Prepared by the sender in `company.yaml`.
3. What we heard: the deal's identified pain, metrics and decision criteria, quoted from the deal record. Leave out any that are empty.
4. How we help: up to three value prop outcomes, the ones whose words best match the pain and metrics.
5. Proof: `company.yaml` proof points only. Leave the section out if there are none.
6. Pricing: line table (item, quantity, unit price, tier, total), then the summary from the pricing method.
7. Terms: payment terms from the price book, currency, quote validity.

A quote that is not ready shows a draft banner and must not be sent.
