# Call scoring rubric (100 points)

| Skill | Points | Full marks when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| talk_ratio | 15 | Rep talks 35% to 60% of the words. Outside the band loses points by distance. |
| questioning | 15 | Rep asks at least the scenario's `min_questions` (default 6). |
| open_questions | 15 | Half or more of the rep's questions are open (what, how, why, walk me through, tell me). |
| monologue | 10 | No rep turn over 120 words. Zero at 250 words or more. |
| qualification | 20 | Buyer evidence for each MEDDPICC element in the scenario `focus` (all eight for a real call). |
| next_step | 10 | A next step is heard (5) and it has a day or date (10). |
| objection_handling | 15 | After each buyer objection the rep acknowledges it, asks a question, and answers with substance. |

If no objection came up and the scenario does not require one, objection_handling scores full marks. If the scenario requires one and none was handled, it scores zero.

The three top fixes are the three skills that lost the most points, each with the measured number and one concrete change.
