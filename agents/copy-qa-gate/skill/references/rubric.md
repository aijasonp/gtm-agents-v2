# Copy rubric (40 points, 32 passes)

| Check | Points | Full marks when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Banned phrases | 10 | None present. Minus 4 per phrase found. |
| Em dashes | 6 | Zero em dashes (or `--` used as one). |
| Sentence length | 6 | Longest sentence 25 words or fewer (3 points up to 35). |
| Readability | 6 | Flesch reading ease 60+ (3 points from 45). |
| Length | 4 | Within the word budget (default 120; 2 points up to 1.5x). |
| Exclamation marks | 2 | None. |
| Unverified claims | 3 | No percentages, multiples ("10x") or guarantees without a named source. |
| Single ask | 3 | Exactly one question (1 point for two). |

Rules behind the rubric:
- One idea, one ask. The reader should know in 5 seconds why you wrote and what you want.
- Specific beats clever: a real detail about them (from a signal) beats any adjective about you.
- Numbers only with a source the rep can name on a call.
