# Rewrite patterns

| Instead of | Write |
| --- | --- |
| I hope this email finds you well. | (delete; open with the reason you wrote) |
| I wanted to reach out because... | Saw {specific detail}. |
| We leverage AI to streamline your workflow. | We cut {task} from {time} to {time} for teams like {peer}. |
| Our cutting-edge, best-in-class platform | (delete the adjectives; name the one thing it does) |
| Would you be open to a quick call to explore synergies? | Worth 15 minutes next week? |
| Just following up on my last email! | {New detail or useful link}. Still relevant? |
| ...which is a game-changer for... | ...which means {concrete outcome}. |
| Two or three questions in one email | Keep the one that matters; cut the rest. |

Em dash fix: split into two sentences, or use a comma or colon.
Long sentence fix: break at "and", "which" or "because".
