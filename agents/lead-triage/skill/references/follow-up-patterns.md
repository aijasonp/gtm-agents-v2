# First follow-up patterns

Goal: a reply or a booked meeting. One idea, one ask, under 110 words.

## Demo request or contact sales (hot)

- Line 1: reference what they asked for, in their words if they wrote a message.
- Line 2: one sentence on the outcome that matches their function (from `company.yaml` value props).
- Ask: two specific times, or the calendar link.

## Event lead

- Line 1: the event by name and what they stopped by for, if known.
- Line 2: one outcome.
- Ask: a 15 minute follow-up next week.

## Webinar or content (warm)

- Line 1: the topic they engaged with.
- Line 2: a question about how they handle it today.
- Ask: whether it is worth comparing notes for 15 minutes.

## Never

- Never mention scores, tiers, tracking, page visits or intent data.
- Never claim a customer result that is not in `company.yaml` proof points.
- Never send to a lead flagged active pipeline. The deal owner handles it.
