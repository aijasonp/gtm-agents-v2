# Pre-call brief format

One brief per upcoming meeting, posted the morning before. Short enough to read in two minutes.

```
Pre-call: Northwind pilot scoping with CFO (Tue Oct 28, 15:00 UTC) | Sam Rivera
Account: Northwind Logistics | Logistics, 600 people, US | Signal Scout A 82
People: Dana Cole (VP of Sales Development, sales), Ana Ruiz (Chief Financial Officer, finance)
Deal: Northwind Logistics - Platform | Evaluation 17 days | $96,000 | close Dec 15
MEDDPICC gaps: Economic buyer (missing), Decision process (missing)
Last call (Oct 20): Pain: reps spend 6 hours a week building lists. Next steps: pilot plan by Friday
History: competitor Acme Intel (1 mention); objections none
Overdue: Share pilot success criteria (due Oct 27)
Plan for the call:
1. Confirm the economic buyer: "Who signs off on a purchase this size?"
2. Map the decision process: "Walk me through how a decision like this gets made, step by step."
3. Leave with a dated next step and an owner.
```

Rules: only facts from the CRM, calls and signals. If something is unknown, write "unknown", never a guess.
