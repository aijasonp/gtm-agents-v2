# Proposal outline

Every section is filled from the deal, MEDDPICC evidence and company.yaml. A section with no evidence is marked "MISSING: ask <question>" so the rep fills it before sending. Never invent a number.

1. **Situation**: the buyer's pain in their words (Identified pain).
2. **Goals and how we will measure them**: Metrics.
3. **Proposed approach**: the value props from company.yaml that match the pain.
4. **Decision criteria we meet**: Decision criteria, one line each.
5. **Timeline**: Decision process plus the close date.
6. **Investment**: deal amount. Terms only if they are on the deal.
7. **Paper process**: legal, security and procurement steps (Paper process).
8. **Next steps**: dated, with owners.
