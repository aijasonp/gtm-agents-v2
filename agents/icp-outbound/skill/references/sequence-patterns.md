# Three-step sequence

Each step under 110 words, one idea, one ask. Each step must pass the Copy QA Gate (32 of 40).

## Step 1: first touch (day 1)

- Line 1: the reason now (the signal's `say_it` line, filled with real detail) or a plain observation about their role.
- Line 2: the outcome from the matching value prop.
- Ask: is this worth 15 minutes next week.

## Step 2: bump (day 4)

- Reply on the same thread. One new angle: the pain behind the value prop, as a question about how they handle it today.
- No stock follow-up openers and no "bumping this"; the Copy QA Gate bans them.

## Step 3: breakup (day 10)

- Short. Say you will stop here, leave the door open, offer a one-line resource or the calendar link.

## Never

- Never mention scores, tracking, page visits or intent data.
- Never claim a result that is not in `company.yaml` proof points.
- Never draft for an account with an open deal.
