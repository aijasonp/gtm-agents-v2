# Daily brief format

One header line, then one line per Tier A and B account, highest score first, at most 20.

```
Signal Scout: 12 accounts to work today
1. Northwind Logistics | A 82 (+9) | New VP of Sales Development, Hiring into the buying team
   Opener angle: Congrats on the new role
2. Contoso Health | A 77 (new) | Raised a funding round, Visited pricing page
   Opener angle: Congrats on the raise
3. Fabrikam Insurance | B 61 (-4) | New regulation applies to them
```

- `(+9)` is the change since the last sweep; `(new)` means it was not ranked before.
- The opener angle comes only from a signal's `say_it` line, filled with real detail. Omit the line when no signal has one.
- No commentary between lines. If there are no A or B accounts, say so in one line.

## Single-account answer

```
Northwind Logistics: Tier A, 82 (ICP 88, heat 78)

Would raise it: a funding, tech-change or intent signal (new category triggers stacking)
```
